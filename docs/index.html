Your privacy

The scripts in this project never send anything anywhere. They run on your computer.

The AI app you choose will read your documents to help you. Its own privacy terms apply, so check them first.

Your documents and results stay in my-documents, which git ignores, so they are never published by accident.

What it cannot do

It cannot tell you for certain that a hospital counts as a temporary workplace. It flags risks. Check with HMRC or an accountant.

It estimates days from working days and leave unless you give it rotas.

It reads ESR payslips only, and does not include Scottish tax bands.

This is a calculation and evidence aid, not tax advice. You are responsible for what you file with HMRC.
