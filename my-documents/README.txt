Put your own documents here. This folder is ignored by git, so nothing in it is ever published.

  payslips/     every payslip PDF you have, from April 2022 onwards
  placements/   your TIS placements page saved as PDF, and ESR assignment pages if you have them
  homes.csv     your home addresses (copy templates/homes.csv and edit)

The tool creates a tax-claim/ folder in here with the results.
